The Pride of Tayabas, Quezon

Established in 1950 in the historic town of Tayabas City, Quezon, Tayabas Bakery has been crafting traditional Filipino pastries and bread for over seven decades. Founded by the Castillo family, the bakery remains a cherished institution in Southern Luzon.

Our famous Special Broas are made using time-honored recipes with fresh eggs and zero artificial preservatives, slow-baked in brick wood-fired ovens ("pugon") to achieve their signature light, airy crunch.
